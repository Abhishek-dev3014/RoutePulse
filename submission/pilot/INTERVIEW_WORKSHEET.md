## Ask about an actual recent journey or operating incident

1. What happened, where, and when?
2. How long did you wait, and how was that time measured or estimated?
3. Did a vehicle pass without boarding you, or did a connection fail?
4. What did you or the operator do next?
5. Which information was missing at that moment?
6. Which constraints would prevent a different departure or vehicle assignment?
7. What would make the proposed change unacceptable?
