# Shadow pilot measurement protocol

Status: proposed; thresholds, campus, dates and operator approval remain unset.

1. Record the campus, responsible operator, data provenance, permitted use and retention period.
2. Freeze calibration/evaluation date ranges before model tuning. Keep later outcomes inaccessible during candidate selection.
3. Confirm constraints with dispatchers and drivers. The synthetic model's fixed dwell, turnaround and outage behavior are assumptions to revisit.
4. Record baseline completion, mean and upper-tail wait, missed transfers, capacity violations, cancellations and ETA error/interval coverage. Document missing observations and denominator definitions.
5. Run proposals in shadow mode. Label counterfactual outcomes as modeled; only actually observed outcomes may be called measured field results.
6. Inspect results by stop, direction and time period. Record transfer and stop-level regressions alongside aggregate changes.
7. Before any real trial, agree thresholds for completion, wait, transfers, cancellations, reliability and operator usability. Record each threshold, owner, rationale and decision rule.
8. Keep operator approval and rollback. A supervised trial requires a separate operating decision and integration work.
