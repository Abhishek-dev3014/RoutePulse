Synthetic voice: macOS Samantha. Spoken over the original, unaltered app walkthrough. All results describe synthetic simulations.

**0.45–13.04 seconds**

Campus journeys should not depend on guesswork. Route Pulse helps operators test better transport plans with the fleet they already have. This demonstration uses a fictional campus and synthetic data.

**13.04–21.89 seconds**

We start a real optimization run. It adjusts departures within allowed windows and reassigns existing vehicles, without adding buses.

**21.89–31.30 seconds**

The proposed plan changes twenty one duties. All ten hard constraint categories pass. The operator can review the changes before accepting a recommendation.

**31.30–45.04 seconds**

For a fair comparison, both plans face the same passengers and traffic. Completed journeys rise from seven hundred and six to eight hundred and forty nine. Average waiting falls from thirty five point three to twenty eight point zero six minutes.

**45.04–61.35 seconds**

One good run is not enough. We freeze this proposal and evaluate it on twenty additional, prespecified synthetic days. The plan is not retuned between days, and the report retains every outcome.

**61.35–79.45 seconds**

Completed journeys improve on all twenty days, averaging about one hundred and fifty additional journeys per day. But there is a trade off: missed connections worsen on thirteen days. Route Pulse makes that limitation visible alongside the gains.

**79.45–94.81 seconds**

Aggregate improvements can hide harm to individual groups. The Evidence Lab breaks down service and waiting time by passenger origin, helping an operator investigate who benefits and who may be worse off.

**94.81–103.68 seconds**

Arrival predictions are checked against simulated ground truth. Reports show measured errors and prediction interval coverage.

**103.68–114.92 seconds**

The next step is a campus shadow pilot using real historical data. This prototype demonstrates a reproducible decision workflow. Benefits in a live campus remain unverified.
